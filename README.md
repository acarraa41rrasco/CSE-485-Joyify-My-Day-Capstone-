Joyify My Day

Building a Private AI-Powered GPS for Lingering Thoughts

Joyify My Day helps users work through lingering thoughts from stressful situations — at work, at school, or in everyday life — by matching what they describe to a guided "Thought-Map" and offering structured, calming guidance.
